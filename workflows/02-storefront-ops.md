# 02, Storefront ops

**Goal:** A storefront that never sleeps, with a human approving everything the customer sees.
**Connectors:** Shopify

## The prompt

> "Watch my Shopify store. Alert me the moment any SKU drops below 10 units, with a restock recommendation based on its 30-day sell rate. Draft product descriptions for new SKUs in my brand voice for my approval before anything goes live. Summarize daily sales every evening at 6pm: revenue, top 3 products, anything unusual."

## Approval checkpoints

- Product descriptions and any customer-facing copy: always approve before publishing.
- Restock orders: agent recommends, owner places (until trust is earned, then approve-by-category).
- Pricing changes: never autonomous. Ever.

## What good looks like

Stockouts drop to zero. New products launch in hours, not weeks. The owner never discovers a problem from a customer first.
