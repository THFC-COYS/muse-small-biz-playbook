# 01, Books on autopilot

**Goal:** Monthly books that close themselves, with the owner approving the exceptions, not doing the data entry.
**Connectors:** QuickBooks (read first, then approve-by-category)

## The prompt

> "You are my bookkeeper. Every Monday, pull last week's transactions from QuickBooks, categorize anything uncategorized, and flag any expense that looks off compared to my trailing 90-day average. Draft the categorization for my approval. Never recategorize anything over $500 without asking me first."

## Approval checkpoints

- New categorizations: approve weekly until the pattern is trusted, then monthly spot-check.
- Anything over $500: always ask.
- Anything that looks like fraud or duplication: surface immediately, don't wait for Monday.

## What good looks like

Month-end close goes from a weekend of receipts to a 15-minute approval session. The owner sees every anomaly. The agent does everything else.
