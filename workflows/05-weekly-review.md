# 05, Weekly ops review

**Goal:** One page every Monday that tells the owner exactly how the business did and what needs them.
**Connectors:** Notion, Asana

## The prompt

> "Every Monday at 7am, build my weekly ops review in Notion: revenue vs target, open Asana tasks past due, top customer issues from the week, cash position, and the three decisions that need me this week. Pull from Shopify, Stripe, QuickBooks, and Slack. One page. If everything is green, say so in one line and don't waste my time."

## Approval checkpoints

- The review itself is read-only: no approvals needed, it's a briefing.
- Any action Muse proposes inside the review: normal approval rules apply.

## What good looks like

The owner starts Monday knowing the state of the business in five minutes. No status meetings. No digging through dashboards. The agent did the reading; you do the deciding.
